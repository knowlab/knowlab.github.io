title: "New Preprint *HH-SAE: Discovering and Steering Hierarchical Knowledge of Complex Manifolds* at [arXiv:2605.10536](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10536). <br/><br/> In high-stakes, high-dimensional data, rare but meaningful signals are often drowned out by dense background context, a problem we call feature density conflict. We propose the Hybrid Hierarchical SAE (HH-SAE), a sparse autoencoder that splits a model's representation into three nested tiers - contextual, atomic and compository. Across several domains it separates broad administrative clinical labels into finer physiological modes, reaches a zero-shot AUC of 0.9156 in cross-domain fraud detection, and loses 13.46% of its utility when the contextual subtraction path is removed. Knowledge-steered synthesis improves AUPRC by 9.9% over leading generative methods, suggesting the model favours high-order mechanistic signals over environmental proxies."

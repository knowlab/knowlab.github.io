title: Paper accepted at EACL 2027 (main conference) *Better Accuracies, Worse Reasoning - A Step-Level Audit of Medical Chain-of-Thought Distillation*. [arXiv 2605.28301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28301). <br/><br/> Our paper, “Better Accuracies, Worse Reasoning - A Step-Level Audit of Medical Chain-of-Thought Distillation”, led by Zhaoyang Jiang, has been accepted to the main conference of EACL 2027 (Athens, 9-14 March 2027). <br/><br/> Distilling a Qwen3-8B student from a DeepSeek-V3-family teacher on medical QA raises MedQA accuracy from 74.7% to 84.4%, yet the step-level error rate of its written reasoning rises from 30.6% to 50.3%. Answer quality and trace factuality move in opposite directions, and hedging language barely changes, so standard confidence signals miss the shift. The pattern holds across other student models and medical benchmarks, and a blinded clinical expert audit shows the same trend. We argue that answer-level evaluation alone is not enough when distilled reasoning traces are released or reused. <br/><br/>Read it at [arXiv 2605.28301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28301).

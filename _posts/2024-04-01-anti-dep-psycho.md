title: Paper published in *British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology* – *Antidepressant and antipsychotic prescribing in patients with type 2 diabetes in Scotland* – at [doi:10.1111/bcp.16171](https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.16171)  <br/><br/> Prescribing of antidepressant and antipsychotic drugs in the UK has increased, but trends in people with type 2 diabetes had not been investigated. We analysed a population-based diabetes registry in Scotland from 2004 to 2021, a period in which the number of people with type 2 diabetes grew from 161,915 to 309,288. Antidepressant prescribing rose from 20.0 to 33.3 per 100 person-years and antipsychotic prescribing from 2.8 to 4.7, across all drug subtypes except first-generation antipsychotics, which stayed largely stable. The rise differed by age, sex and socioeconomic status.<br/><br/>Read the full paper at [doi:10.1111/bcp.16171](https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.16171).

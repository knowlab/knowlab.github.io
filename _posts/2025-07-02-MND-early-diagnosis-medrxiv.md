title: "New Preprint *Characteristics and Early Diagnosis of Motor Neuron Disease (MND) in 67 million individuals in England: a comparative study on phenotyping models derived by AI, Knowledge Graphs and the MND Association* at [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.01.25330428v1). <br/><br/> Early diagnosis of motor neuron disease (MND) matters because the disease progresses quickly, yet it remains hard to achieve. Using linked health records of 67 million people in England, we compared the MND Association Red Flag guideline with three data-driven approaches - a knowledge graph (PrimeKG), GPT-4 and machine learning on real-world records - for diagnosing MND and for predicting it 1, 3 and 5 years before diagnosis. The guideline alone gave poor to moderate discrimination, and combining it with the AI-derived approaches improved early prediction. The hypothesis-free real-world-data approach identified 13 novel phenotypes from 7 ICD-10 chapters that significantly predicted MND. We also found that people with MND had a higher risk of COVID-19 death in the first wave, while the excess risk in fully vaccinated people in the second wave was not statistically significant."

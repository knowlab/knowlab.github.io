title: Paper published in *Scientific Reports* – *Prevalence of 406 rare diseases by ethnicity and recorded COVID-19 infection rates among 62.5 million people in England* – at [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-70531-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-70531-4)  <br/><br/> We estimated how common 406 rare diseases are across ethnic groups in England, and how recorded COVID-19 infection varied among people with these diseases, using linked NHS England records for 62.5 million people alive on 31 July 2023. About 1.05 million people (1.7%) had at least one of the rare diseases. Recorded COVID-19 infection was higher among them than in the wider population (32.8% vs 29.0%). Both prevalence and infection rates varied by ethnic group and by specific condition, and using detailed NHS ethnicity categories revealed differences that broad categories hide. The findings can support earlier, fairer diagnosis and more targeted care. <br/><br/>Read the full paper at [doi:10.1038/s41598-026-70531-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-70531-4).

## The question

During labour, cardiotocography (CTG) tracks the baby's heart rate to spot a lack of oxygen. Reading it is hard and varies between clinicians. Could machine learning help?

## What we did

We searched PubMed, EMBASE and IEEE Xplore and reviewed 36 studies that used signal processing and machine learning on CTG.

## What we found

Studies used many ways to extract signals and many algorithms. Most used one open-access database and treated fetal acidosis as the benchmark for hypoxia, with differing pH cut-offs, which we flagged as a concern. That database has few subjects for machine learning.

## Why it matters

ML shows potential, but progress to the clinic needs more diverse data, standard definitions of hypoxia and better algorithms and features.

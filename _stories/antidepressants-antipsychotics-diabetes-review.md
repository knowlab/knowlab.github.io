## The question

Many people with type 2 diabetes are prescribed antidepressants or antipsychotics. Do these medicines affect how their diabetes goes?

## What we did

As part of a PhD project, we carried out a systematic review, which means searching and appraising the research in a structured way, of observational studies on antidepressant or antipsychotic prescribing and diabetes outcomes.

## What we found

Studies on this question are scarce, have shortcomings and give mixed findings. There is not enough evidence yet to say how these drugs affect diabetes outcomes.

## Why it matters

Until better evidence arrives, people with diabetes who are prescribed these medicines should receive the monitoring, risk-factor treatment and complication screening recommended in general diabetes guidelines.

## The question

Early in the pandemic there were worries that some common blood pressure drugs might raise the risk of catching COVID-19, and hopes that others might lower it. Real-world records are messy, and people on different drugs are not directly comparable.

## What we did

We used a transformer model, the same family of technology as modern language models, to estimate counterfactual outcomes, which means what would have happened to the same person under a different treatment. We applied it to real-world data from more than 300,000 patients.

## What we found

ACE inhibitors showed a negligible effect on COVID-19 risk. Beta-blockers and calcium channel blockers were associated with a protective effect, while statins and thiazides were associated with a slight increase in risk.

## Why it matters

This is a proof-of-concept study, so these results are not a reason for anyone to change their medication. What it shows is that this kind of AI-based causal analysis can help evaluate treatments in messy real-world data.

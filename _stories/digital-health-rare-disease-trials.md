## The question

Hundreds of millions of people live with rare diseases, and most of these conditions have no effective treatment. New treatments need clinical trials, but trials for rare diseases are hard. Patients are few and spread far apart, and taking part over a long time is difficult.

## What we did

We carried out a systematic review of how digital health tools, such as wearable devices, mobile apps and healthcare platforms, are used in rare disease trials. We analysed 262 trials across ten rare diseases.

## What we found

Digital technologies were increasingly used for remote monitoring, digital treatment and long-term care.

## Why it matters

Digital tools make it easier for patients to take part wherever they live. That can speed up the development of treatments and make research more inclusive by reaching patients who would otherwise be left out.

## The question

Prescribing of antidepressants and antipsychotics has risen in the UK. Nobody had looked at trends in people with type 2 diabetes.

## What we did

We analysed a population-based diabetes register in Scotland year by year from 2004 to 2021. The number of people with type 2 diabetes grew from 161,915 to 309,288.

## What we found

Antidepressant prescribing rose from 20.0 to 33.3 per 100 person-years, and antipsychotics from 2.8 to 4.7. This held for all drug subtypes except first-generation antipsychotics, which stayed stable. Patterns differed by age, sex and deprivation.

## Why it matters

The rise may reflect growing multimorbidity. Next, we need the reasons, and whether it is more new prescriptions or longer use.
